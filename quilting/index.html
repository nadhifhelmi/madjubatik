Questions from quilters

In Bali? Come and choose in person at Jl. Sulawesi No. 19, Denpasar, across the road from Pasar Badung.

Where can I buy Bali batik jelly rolls?

From our shop on Jl. Sulawesi No. 19, Denpasar, Bali, or by WhatsApp from anywhere in the world. Our strip rolls are 2.5″ × 44″ batik cotton strips, rolled and tied by hand.

What sizes are your quilting packs?

Strips 2.5″ × 44″, squares 25 × 25 cm, minis 5″ × 5″, fat quarter cuts 45 × 55 cm, and scraps in mixed sizes.

What fabric are the packs cut from?

Our quilting packs are cut from batik cotton, the same crisp cotton on the left wall of the shop.

Do you ship quilting packs abroad?

Yes, we ship worldwide by air or by sea. Air is faster; sea takes longer but costs less for big orders. Message us on WhatsApp for a shipping quote.

Can I choose the colours?

Each pack mixes a range of shades in one colour family, light to dark. Tell us the colours you're after and we'll send photos of what's ready this week.

Can I buy batik cotton by the metre for backing and borders?

Yes. We cut batik cotton to your length, and for 45 metres or more of one print we can order from our sample books.

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp at +62 878-6223-6936. We'll send photos of the packs we have now.
