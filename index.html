Madju is the old spelling of maju: forward. You'll find it on our sign on Jalan Sulawesi, Denpasar's fabric street.

Every wall is sorted by colour, so your blue is one look away.

Cut to your length at the counter, then folded and packed by hand.

The shop cats keep watch over the cotton wall.

What we sell

Batik by the metre in three fabrics, packs cut for patchwork, and bulk orders.

  • Crisp and easy to sew. Our biggest range, for quilting, shirts and craft. The whole left wall of the shop.

  • Soft, with a heavy drape. Made for dresses, sarongs and loose summer clothes.

  • Our lightest fabric, airy and slightly sheer. Only a few prints in right now, so message us to check what's in.

  • Stacks of cuts tied with ribbon, sorted into runs of one colour from light to dark.

  • Jelly-roll style strips, rolled by hand. Each roll mixes a range of colours. See the packs.

  • Squares of 25 × 25 cm, minis of 5 × 5 inches, and scraps in mixed sizes for appliqué and small projects.

  • Hundreds more prints from our sample books, made to order. How bulk orders work.

Good to know

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you ship overseas?
Yes, by air or by sea. Sea is slower but costs less for big orders. Message us on WhatsApp with what you'd like and your country, and we'll send you a shipping quote.
How do I order?
Message us on WhatsApp at +62 878-6223-6936. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll send photos of what's in stock.
How do I pay?
Most customers pay by bank transfer or with Wise.
How wide is the fabric?
Batik cotton and batik rayon are 112–115 cm wide. Rayon voile is 140 cm wide.
